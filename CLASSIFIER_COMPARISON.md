## 🔍 **Detailed Analysis**

### **Rule-Based Classifier Approach:**
- **Method**: Keyword matching + regex patterns
- **Keywords**: Looks for exact matches like "EUV", "GAA", "sub-7nm"
- **Confidence**: Calculated by counting keyword matches
- **Evidence**: Extracts sentences containing keywords
- **Limitations**: 
  - Misses implied capabilities
  - Low confidence on complex descriptions
  - No contextual understanding
  - Rigid pattern matching

### **AI Classifier Approach:**
- **Method**: Natural language understanding + technical analysis
- **Analysis**: Understands context, relationships, and implications
- **Confidence**: Based on technical depth and evidence quality
- **Evidence**: Provides detailed technical reasoning
- **Advantages**:
  - Understands implied capabilities
  - High confidence on technical content
  - Contextual analysis
  - Flexible interpretation

## 🚀 **Recommendation**

### **Use AI Classifier When:**
- ✅ High accuracy is critical
- ✅ You want to discover hidden capabilities
- ✅ Cost is acceptable (~$0.03 per entity)
- ✅ You have complex technical descriptions
- ✅ You want detailed evidence and insights

### **Use Rule-Based Classifier When:**
- ✅ Cost is a primary concern
- ✅ You have simple, explicit technical descriptions
- ✅ Speed is critical (instant results)
- ✅ You want predictable, consistent results

### **Hybrid Approach (Recommended):**
- Use AI for high-value entities (>$50M funding)
- Use rule-based for simple cases
- Fall back to rule-based if AI fails
- Cost: ~$0.50-1.50 per month for your current scale
